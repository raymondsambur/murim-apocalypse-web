### What must exist (starting numbers; tune with soak tests)
**Clock**: `totalHours` advances via `advanceHours(n, nowMs)`; 24 h/day, 30 days/season, 4 seasons, year = 120 days. `timeView(nowMs)` returns fractional hours (anchored to the last advance) and weather for the 3 climate bands.
**Weather** per climate band (0 north, 1 central, 2 south), re-rolled daily from a season-aware Markov chain (snow only in winter and mainly north, storms in summer/autumn, fog in spring/autumn). `weatherAt(x,y)` uses the band of y (north y<30% of height, south y>68%).
**Blight**: Uint8Array (w*h) 0..255. Initial: dense around the 3 hearts (up to ~200, falling off to ~0 by ~16 tiles, with noise), light around the ruin. Each hour: emission from active hearts into their neighbourhood; diffusion into passable land neighbours (water/mountain stay 0); slow decay; suppression near defended settlements (radius ~ settlement radius+6 scaled by `defense`/status; fallen settlements stop suppressing and become growth sinks); rain -25%, winter -40%, summer +20%. Hearts: `strength` 0..100, regrow slowly unless sealed; `damageHeart` reduces strength and can seal for several days (chronicle line naming the player). `purify(x,y,radius,amount)`; `recordKill(x,y,family,level,elite)` lowers local Blight slightly for blight-family kills. Must be dynamic but **bounded**: over 90 world days with no player input it visibly spreads and pulses, doesn't flood the continent nor die out; settlements near the ruin are under pressure; the starting village stays safe for a long time. Emit `{k:'blight', idx}` for changed tiles (if >4000 changed, one event with all indices).
**Settlements** (`SettlementState`): initial controllers by faction `homeKind` (azure -> sectHall, tiger -> fort, jade -> town nearest the start, crimson -> the other town, blackwind -> a village/hamlet far from the start; village 0 independent); ruin starts `fallen`. Update `prosperity`, `defense` (base by kind; + faction power share; - Blight pressure), `population`, `blight` (avg nearby), `status` with hysteresis (thriving/stable/struggling/besieged/fallen). **Sieges**: triggered by high Blight or an enemy faction raid; `siegeUntil`/`siegeStrength`; `{k:'siege', start}` events; defense vs strength decides: success keeps the town, failure -> `fallen` (population crash, controller null, chronicle importance 3). Fallen settlements can be retaken when Blight is low and a faction has power, or after many quiet days. Flags: `famine`, `plague`, `festival`, `boom`.
**Economy**: goods = `GOODS`. Per settlement `stock/prod/cons` (units/day) from population, kind, season (grain x0.25 in winter, x1.5 autumn), controller (Jade boosts trade), Blight (reduces prod), siege (stops trade). Daily update; **caravans** move surplus along `world.roads` unless the road is too blighted or an end is besieged/fallen. Shortage (< ~3 days of consumption) sets `famine` and decays population/prosperity. `priceMult(settlementId,itemId)`: goods `clamp((target/(stock+1))^0.5, 0.6, 2.5)` (target ~10 days of consumption), other items ~1.0 with small modifiers (weapons dearer under siege/war, medicine dearer in plague). `recordTrade` shifts stock.
**Factions** (5 from `data/factions.ts`): power, wealth, influence, relations (start from `initialRelation`), `atWarWith`, `alliedWith`, `capital`, `goal`. Daily AI picks a weighted action: develop, recruit, fight Blight near owned settlements, raid enemy/neutral settlement (relation < -30 or opportunity; power vs defense; capture or plunder; chronicle), diplomacy (alliance > 60, war < -60; wars end by exhaustion or when the Blight threat is high), caravans (Jade). Limit major actions to ~1 per 1-3 days per faction. Must be stable long-run (no faction pinned at 0/100 forever, no runaway winner within 360 days, factions can lose and recover settlements).
**NPC roster** (created at init, deterministic): per settlement (skip the ruin): 2-3 guards (fort 4), 1 elder, 1 merchant (shop 'general'), 1 smith (shop 'weapons') in towns/villages/forts, 1 alchemist (shop 'medicine') in towns/villages/sectHall, 1 innkeeper where an inn exists, 3-6 villagers, occasional wanderers; **masters** (role 'master', `faction` = controller or nearest by school) in the sectHall (3-4), fort (2-3), towns (1-2); `teaches` 2-4 skills of the faction's `schools` with `req.rank <=` the master's rank; levels: village masters 8-18, towns 14-30, sect/fort 20-40; **legends** (role 'legend', `legend:true`, `legendTitle`) about one per two settlements at levels 36-60, sometimes wandering (`npcMoved`). Names `personName`, looks `randomLook`, `homeX/homeY` = a passable tile near the relevant building door (smith near 'smithy', alchemist 'alchemy', innkeeper 'inn', merchant 'market', guards near gates/plaza, masters near 'dojo'/'hall'/'pagoda') using shared `isPassable`. Unique ids from 1 (`nextNpcId`).
**NPC lives** (daily): aging (1 year = 120 days), training xp -> level, death by old age (>~65 with rising odds), Blight raids on fallen/besieged settlements, feuds between masters of warring factions, plague. Death emits `{k:'npcDied'}` + chronicle (importance 2-3 for masters/legends). **Succession**: masters replaced by a promoted villager/newcomer (`npcBorn`, inherits `teaches`, chronicle "X takes the mantle of Master Y"); shopkeepers replaced so services never vanish; legends can rise (master reaching rank 4-5 gains a title) and fall. Roster size stays stable.
**Missions**: each settlement keeps 4-8 offers on its board (`boards[settlementId]`), refreshed daily and on relevant events, generated from world state with valid `MissionOffer` fields (`id` = `m${nextOfferId++}`, type, title, desc in flavourful wuxia voice, settlementId, target, minRank, level, reward, expires (world day)). Types: **hunt** (monster id from `MONSTERS` matching local danger, count 3-8), **gather** (herb/ore_iron/hide/meat/fang counts; bigger reward when that good is scarce), **deliver** (item+count to another connected settlement via `target.toSettlement`, bonus if it's in famine), **purify** (kill N blight monsters near `target.x,y,radius`, picked at high-Blight spots near living settlements), **defend** (only during an active siege), **bounty** (named elite/boss: `target.monster`, `target.elite` 1|2), **investigate** (visit x,y: ruins, shrines, heart perimeter). Level = local danger level like `levelForDanger` (settlement 0 offers level 1-4 only). Rewards: xp ~ 25+level*9, gold ~ 12+level*6, faction `rep` +2..+8, occasional items (pills, `manual_<skillId>` for skills of tier <= local rank). Provide `takeOffer`, `returnOffer`, `recordMissionDone` (boosts the settlement; chronicle for notable ones).
**Chronicle**: ring buffer of 300; short English lines with `day, hour, kind, importance, settlementId?`. Examples: "The Black Wind Bandits seized Stone Brook from the Tiger Fist Clan.", "Famine grips Cloud Crossing.", "Master Han Muyeong of the Azure Cloud Sect has died of old age.", "The Blight has reached the outskirts of Azure Pass.", "Winter has come." Pace/dedupe to ~4-12 entries per world day, not hundreds.
**Other `Sim` methods**: `settlementView(s)`, `worldInfo`, `npcs/npc/greeting/shopFor` ('general' = food/pills/basic gear; 'weapons' = tier 1-3 weapons+outfits by prosperity; 'medicine' = healing/qi pills, antidote, low-tier breakthrough pills; `sellMult` 0.4-0.5), `record`, `serialize` / restore via `opts.saved` (lossless incl. Rng state and blight), `state`.
