### Required content
**Projection**: isometric 2:1, tile 64x32: `sx=(x-y)*32`, `sy=(x+y)*16`. Painter's-algorithm depth sorting by `x+y`. Camera smoothly follows the local player (`setSelf`), zoom 0.5-2.5 (wheel/pinch), drag-pan detaches temporarily (re-attach on recenter or when the player moves). Handle devicePixelRatio and resize.
**Terrain**: every code in `T` with 3-4 deterministic variants per tile position, soft edge blending (grass->dirt->sand->water), shoreline foam, cliff faces for Mountain (Rock) with snow caps in the north, highlands, forest floor, snowfield, sand, farmland rows (crops change with season), roads (dirt ruts, stone pavers), plaza, bridges (planks over water), ruins (broken flagstones). **Seasons** via `TimeView.season`: spring (blossoms/light green), summer (deep green), autumn (orange/red, golden fields), winter (snow dusting on grass/roofs/trees, ice on shallow water). Water is animated (shimmer) and drawn per frame only for visible tiles; static ground is cached in chunk canvases (e.g. 16x16 tiles), redrawn only on season/Blight change.
**Objects** (`O`): pine, oak (seasonal foliage), bamboo cluster, boulder, bush, reeds, dead tree, bones, flowers, banner. **Buildings** from `world.payload.buildings` by kind: house (timber + tiled hip roof, upturned eaves), inn (two storeys + lanterns), smithy (forge glow, chimney smoke), alchemy (cauldron, hanging herbs, green lanterns), market (striped awning stalls), shrine (red gate/small shrine), pagoda (3-5 tiers), hall (large temple hall), dojo (open hall with weapon rack), farmhouse, herbHut, mineEntrance (timber-framed tunnel mouth on a walkable tile), tower, gate, barracks, ruinedHouse (collapsed, charred), warehouse, teahouse; **fort walls** (`O.Wall`) connecting orthogonally with crenellations. Faction colour accents on banners/roof ridge by controller from `setSettlements`; fallen settlements look burnt/overgrown. Footprints are `w x d` tiles from (x,y); draw each building as one sprite sorted by its front-most tile.
**Blight overlay** (`setBlight`/`patchBlight`, 0..255 per tile): corrupted ground tint (desaturate + purple/black), withered vegetation above a threshold (dead trees), creeping dark mist wisps and pulsing violet veins above ~120, strongest near hearts. Cheap, cached per chunk (update chunks when patched). Ominous but beautiful.
**Day/night & weather** (`setTime`): smooth tint across the 24 h clock (dawn warm, noon neutral, dusk orange, night deep blue) with lantern glows from buildings at night; weather for the climate band of the local player's position: clear, cloudy, rain (streaks + splashes), storm (heavy rain, wind, lightning flashes), fog (layered), snow (flakes). Cheap particles.
**Entities** (`applyEntities`): interpolate movement `x,y` -> `nx,ny` over `step` ms; facing `face` (0 SE, 1 SW, 2 NW, 3 NE).
- **Characters** (players/NPCs/human monsters) from `Look` indices; `lookSwatches` exports CSS colours with lengths equal to `LOOK_RANGES`: skin tones, 7 hair styles (short, topknot, long loose, ponytail, braided, shaven, hat-hair), 8 hair colours (incl. grey/white), 4 outfit cuts (commoner tunic, scholar robe, warrior jacket, long sect robe), 10 outfit colours, 8 sash colours, 5 accessories (none, headband, bamboo hat, half mask, hairpin). Gender subtly shapes the silhouette. **Layered paper-doll** sprite (~24x40 base pixels, scaled), 4 directions (front/back + mirror), animations: idle (breathing), walk (4 frames), fight (stance + swing, 3 frames), meditate (seated, qi glow), work (hammer/hoe), dead (lying). Held weapon from `weapon` (sword, blade, spear, staff, fist wraps). Cache frames per (look, weapon, state, dir, frame) in a bounded LRU. `role` adds props/tints (guard armour + spear, merchant hat, master white robe + faction sash, legend aura).
- **Monsters** by `sprite`: `boar, wolf, snake, bear, tiger, bandit, bandit_boss, hollow, wolf_blight, hollow_monk, hollow_brute, hollow_lord` (humans use the paper-doll with special palettes: bandits dark with face cloth, hollows pale grey-green with hollow eyes and dripping violet corruption; bosses larger with aura); `elite` 1/2 adds glow/size.
- **Nodes** by `nodeKind`: `herb` (green tuft), `rareHerb` (glowing), `ore` (rocky vein with glints), `shrine` (stone lantern shrine with light), `heart` (huge pulsing dark crystal/blighted tree heart with swirling particles - the visual climax of the apocalypse).
- **Overlays**: ground shadows; name plates (name, optional `tag`, colour by rank/kind; hostile red, npc gold/green, self highlight); HP bars for monsters and entities in combat; floating combat text from `combatEvents` (damage white, crit larger yellow, heal green, "Miss" grey, dot small coloured, status icons) rising and fading; hit flashes / slash arcs; `fx` kinds (levelup golden pillar+rings with text, breakthrough lightning burst, death soul-wisp, loot sparkle, heal green motes, learn book glow, gather puff, purify ring); speech bubbles via `say`; selection ring (`setSelected`); move-target marker with ripple (`setMoveTarget`).
- **Picking**: `onPick` returns tile + topmost entity (sprite bounds + small tolerance); mouse left = tap, right = context; touch tap = tap, long press (500 ms) = context; hover (throttled). Dragging pans without firing a tap.
**Minimap** (`drawMinimap`): cached terrain colours, roads, settlement markers + names (fallen = red mark, besieged = pulsing), Blight overlay, nearby entities as dots (self bright, monsters red, players blue, npcs yellow), camera frustum rectangle; `minimapToTile` for clicks.
**`drawLookPreview(canvas, look, opts)`** and `lookSwatches` for character creation (large, crisp, idle breathing when `time` is provided).
